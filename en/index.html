FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does my travel companion need to install the app?

No. The person inviting a guest uses LingoOasis. The guest scans a QR code and joins in a browser.

Which languages are supported?

Japanese, English, Chinese, Korean, Thai, Vietnamese, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Russian, and Hindi.

Can we chat without internet access?

Yes, with translation data prepared ahead of time. The app user sets up local Wi-Fi or a hotspot, and the guest’s device must connect to the same network.

What do I need to prepare for offline translation?

An internet connection and available device storage are needed for the initial download. We recommend preparing the translation data over Wi-Fi before your trip.
